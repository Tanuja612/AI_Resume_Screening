About the Project

📋 Project Overview

The AI Resume Screening and Interview Evaluation System is a comprehensive recruitment platform designed to streamline the entire hiring process. It provides candidates with professional resume building tools, ATS optimization, and enables recruiters to conduct intelligent resume screening with both text-based and voice-based interviews.

This system helps both job seekers and recruiters by offering resume creation with professional templates, automated ATS compatibility improvements, detailed resume analysis, and innovative virtual voice interviews (similar to Alexa/Siri). It leverages AI and natural language processing to make the hiring process efficient, objective, and modern.

⚡ Key Features

1. 📝 Resume Builder with Templates

  • Create professional resumes using 4 pre-designed templates (Professional, Chronological, Functional, Modern)
  • Step-by-step guided resume creation wizard
  • Sections include: Contact Info, Professional Summary, Skills, Work Experience, Education, Projects, Certifications, Languages
  • Dynamic entry addition for multiple work experiences, education, and projects
  • Live preview of resume as you build it
  • Download created resume immediately after creation

2. ✅ ATS Compatibility Check & Optimization

  • Verify resume formatting for ATS system compatibility with detailed scoring (0-100)
  • Identify specific issues: tabs, columns, special characters, keyword stuffing, missing sections
  • One-click improvement button that generates ATS-optimized version
  • Shows specific corrections applied: removed tabs, fixed spacing, cleaned line breaks, standardized bullets
  • Download corrected resume with detailed list of fixes
  • Before/after score comparison
  • Strengths, weaknesses, and recommendations provided

3. 🎤 Virtual Voice Interviews

  • AI-powered voice interviews similar to Alexa/Siri interaction
  • Uses browser's Speech Recognition API for voice input
  • Text-to-speech for question delivery
  • Real-time transcript display of candidate responses
  • Animated waveform visualization during recording
  • Microphone permission handling and browser compatibility detection
  • Fallback options for unsupported browsers

4. 💬 Text-Based Interviews

  • Traditional Q&A interview with AI-generated questions
  • Questions based on resume content and job description
  • Automated scoring of written responses
  • Interview score combination with resume score

5. 🔍 Smart Resume Screening

  • Upload and process multiple resumes (PDF, TXT, DOCX formats)
  • Intelligent text extraction and preprocessing
  • Resume ranking based on job description match
  • Detailed scoring with keyword matching analysis
  • Support for both uploaded and created resumes

6. 👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard

  • Monitor all registered users and candidates
  • View candidate final scores and interview results
  • Selection status tracking (Selected/Rejected/Pending)
  • Action buttons to mark candidates as selected or rejected
  • View detailed resume information and analysis
  • User activity tracking and analytics

7. 🏠 User Dashboard

  • Welcome page after login with personalized greeting
  • Quick access to resume creation or upload
  • View recent resumes and their scores
  • Track resume submission history

🚀 How It Works

For Job Seekers:

Path 1: Create Resume from Scratch

  • Register and login to your account
  • Click "Create Resume" on dashboard
  • Choose from 4 professional templates
  • Fill in your information step-by-step
  • View live preview as you build
  • Save and download your resume
  • Check ATS compatibility and get corrections if needed
  • Download optimized version

Path 2: Upload Existing Resume

  • Upload resume from your device
  • System checks ATS compatibility
  • View detailed analysis with strengths and weaknesses
  • Generate improved ATS-friendly version with specific fixes
  • Download corrected resume

For Recruiters:

Full Screening Process

  • Receive candidate applications with resumes
  • System scores resumes based on job description
  • View ranked candidates from highest to lowest match
  • Conduct interviews: Text-based Q&A or Voice-based
  • Get combined resume + interview scores
  • Mark candidates as Selected/Rejected in admin dashboard
  • Download comprehensive evaluation reports

The Interview Process:

  • Candidate chooses between Text Interview or Voice Interview
  • AI generates questions based on resume and job description
  • Voice Interview: Candidate speaks responses, system records and analyzes
  • Text Interview: Candidate types responses with scoring feedback
  • Final score combines resume match score with interview score
  • Results displayed with detailed feedback

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a resume?

Login to your account, go to Dashboard, click "Create Resume". Choose one of 4 templates, fill in your information step-by-step, and save. You can download it immediately or check ATS compatibility first.

What file formats are supported for upload?

We support PDF, TXT, and DOCX formats. For best results, ensure your resume is clearly formatted in one of these formats.

How does ATS checking and correction work?

Our system analyzes your resume for ATS compatibility issues like tabs, columns, special characters, and keyword stuffing. Click "Suggest improvements" to generate a corrected version with specific fixes listed. Download the optimized resume.

How does the voice interview work?

The voice interview is like talking to an AI assistant (similar to Alexa/Siri). The system asks questions aloud, you speak your answers, and your responses are recorded and analyzed. You'll see a transcript of what was recorded.

What's the difference between voice and text interviews?

Text Interview: Traditional Q&A where you type answers. Voice Interview: You speak your answers using your microphone. Both are scored and contribute to your final evaluation.

How accurate is the resume scoring?

Our AI identifies keywords, skills, and experience relevant to the job description. The accuracy depends on how well your resume content matches the job requirements. ATS scoring checks technical formatting compatibility.

Can I download my resume after creation?

Yes! After creating a resume, you'll see a success page with a download button. You can also download ATS-corrected versions after optimization.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all user data is encrypted and stored securely. We follow industry best practices for data protection and privacy.

Do I need a microphone for the voice interview?

Yes, the voice interview requires a microphone and browser support for the Web Speech API (supported in Chrome, Edge, Firefox). You can also choose the text interview as an alternative.
